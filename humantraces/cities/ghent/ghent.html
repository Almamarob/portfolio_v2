Ghent

Shaped by its historical heritage and a fiercely independent spirit, Ghent redefines contemporary living by turning waterfront regeneration and ecological transition into a model of social cohesion, creativity, and high quality of life.

Urban Matrix

Spatial Morphology

A polycentric, compact Flemish city structured around the confluence of the Scheldt and Lys rivers. The pedestrianized medieval historic center (one of the largest in Europe) is enclosed by a ring of 19th-century and contemporary districts. Urban regeneration is concentrated on the northern axis of the city port (Oude Dokken) and former industrial and railway areas (Sint-Pieters), reconnected by pedestrian-cycle bridges and new tram lines. The mobility plan (Circulatieplan) eliminated through-traffic, returning the street to people.

City Layout & Building Typologies

High stratification. Historic stone buildings and corporate palaces in the center guarantee prestige but carry strict conservation constraints; typical Flemish brick bel-étage and terraced houses (rijteshuizen) characterize residential districts; modular timber and circular architecture dominate the regeneration of disused port areas.

Living Culture & Social Anchors

Commercially vibrant ground floors host organic markets, independent cafés, and creative spaces. The street is conceived as an extended social space (leefstraten — "livable streets"), where during the warm season traffic is suspended to make room for tables, temporary greenery, and play areas.

Public Space, Urban Voids & Nature

Water is the structuring element of the city's identity. Canal remediation, riverbank renaturalization, and the creation of integrated urban parks (e.g., Brielmeersen, Gentbrugse Meersen) guide climate-response planning. Urban voids and disused industrial sites are given value through temporary cultural uses before their final transformation.

Demographics & Social Composition

A dynamic, young population, marked by a very high percentage of university students (Ghent University) and young professionals in the creative and biotech industries. Demand is concentrated on functional apartments, renovated terraced houses, and flexible housing solutions.

Market Characteristics

Urban Model: Flanders' second economic hub, Ghent offers a stable, highly attractive real estate market with housing demand structurally exceeding supply, ensuring steady returns and low vacancy risk.

Investor Profile: A balanced presence of Belgian/European institutional capital (focused on the Build-to-Rent and student housing segments) and local private investors. Strong weight of sustainable development companies operating in partnership with public authorities.

Construction Culture: Rigorous focus on circular construction, reuse of demolition materials, and energy efficiency (BEN — Bijna Energieneutraal, "nearly energy-neutral"). BIM adoption and timber prefabrication technologies are widely used in waterfront regeneration projects.

Regulation: A regulatory framework oriented toward heritage protection and sustainable densification. Strict municipal urban plans requiring high ESG performance, reduced land consumption (betonstop), and social or regulated housing quotas in large developments.

Tech Adoption: An innovation hub for cleantech and sustainable materials. Use of PropTech systems for monitoring district heating networks and solutions for integrating shared renewable energy.

What Makes Ghent Different

In Ghent, innovation and capital must integrate with a strong, progressive community spirit. Investments succeed when they respect local identity and contribute to the city's environmental sustainability.

  • Waterfront Regeneration: The redevelopment of former port areas (Oude Dokken) demonstrates how high-value densification can coexist with the creation of new, accessible, ecological public spaces.
  • Strict ESG Standards: Energy efficiency, integration with the cycling network, and circular resource management are not optional, but necessary conditions for project approval.
  • Long-Term Stability: A market driven by solid university and professional demand ensures rental values hold steady, sheltered from cyclical fluctuations.
  • Collaborative Planning: Major interventions require strong coordination among developers, administration, and residents to secure social acceptance of the project.

Resident Perspective

Market Characteristics

Urban Model: A human-scale city where cycling mobility and the public transport network allow any point in the city to be reached in under 15 minutes, ensuring a relaxed, sustainable lifestyle.

Investor Profile (How the market is experienced): A competitive residential market with a high percentage of owner-occupiers and long-term tenants. Growing interest in cohousing, housing cooperatives, and community renovations.

Construction Culture: Properties characterized by careful attention to thermal efficiency, use of natural materials, and flexible interior layouts. Constant presence of inner courtyards, terraces, or herringbone-pattern gardens.

Regulation: Protections aimed at preserving the city's affordability, containing speculation, and ensuring high quality standards for both private rentals and social housing.

Tech Adoption: Technology oriented toward improving daily life: shared mobility apps, home automation systems for energy consumption management, and local participatory platforms.

What Makes Ghent Different

An urban culture grounded in cultural vibrancy, environmental respect, and community life.

  • ESG as Everyday Comfort: Sustainability translates into car-free neighborhoods, clean air, widespread urban greenery, and swimmable canals.
  • Progressive Community: An open, inclusive atmosphere oriented toward social innovation, where grassroots initiatives often find institutional support.
  • Active Waterfront: Canals and riverbanks are not merely scenic elements, but genuine extensions of the domestic living room for leisure time.
  • Work-Life Balance: The city's pace allows professional life to be balanced with leisure, culture, and neighborhood social life.
